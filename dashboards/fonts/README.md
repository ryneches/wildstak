# Fonts

Both are pixel fonts, used only at their native sizes (see DASHBOARD_SPEC.md).
The firmware's status screen uses MaruMonica too.

## x12y16pxMaruMonica (マルモニカ)

Body text: device labels, ticks, values. Copyright (c) 2021 hicc (患者長ひっく).
Source: [hicchicc.github.io/00ff](https://hicchicc.github.io/00ff/)
(also on [BOOTH](https://00ff.booth.pm/items/2958237)).

Distributed under hicc's own license, not a standard one. Its terms, as
published on the source page (checked 2026-11-14):

- 加工・改変・再配布も可能となっていますが、フォントの著作権は放棄しておりません。
  (Modification and redistribution are allowed; hicc keeps the copyright.)
- .ttfファイルを同梱してのゲームリリースや、商用利用も可能です。
  (Bundling the .ttf with a release, and commercial use, are allowed.)
- フォント利用の際の出典・クレジット表記などは、して頂いても、しなくても、どちらでも構いません。
  (Credit is optional.)
- Selling the font itself is prohibited.

hicc is moving their fonts to the SIL OFL; MaruMonica wasn't yet when
checked. Copies already obtained stay usable under the terms they came with.
